## Why it matters

Buxton makes a crucial distinction between refining a design and discovering which design deserves refinement. Sketches create the ambiguity, speed, and low cost needed for the second kind of work.

## Best for

Best for product and interaction designers, researchers, and teams that move to polished interfaces before exploring enough alternatives.

## What you'll learn

You will learn what makes a sketch useful, how to externalize interaction ideas, and how representation influences collaboration and decision-making.
