## Why it matters

This broad history gives the collection chronological scaffolding. It helps individual monographs and movements sit within longer changes in writing, printing, photography, media, and professional practice.

## Best for

Best for students, teachers, and designers who need a dependable reference for placing visual work in historical context.

## What you'll learn

You will trace recurring relationships among technology, patronage, culture, and form while building a larger map of the field's major movements and practitioners.
