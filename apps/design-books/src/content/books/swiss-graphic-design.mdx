## Why it matters

The collection contains several Swiss classics; Hollis supplies the connective history around them. He shows that the international style emerged from networks of teachers, clients, printers, and political conditions rather than a single formula.

## Best for

Best for readers who know the visual language of Swiss design and want to understand how, where, and why it developed.

## What you'll learn

You will connect canonical artifacts to the schools, debates, technologies, and commissions that made their influence possible.
