## Why it matters

Young offers a rigorous way to move from interview data to a durable view of people's reasoning. The resulting models help teams look beyond feature requests and organize decisions around real cognitive patterns.

## Best for

Best for design researchers, strategists, and product teams planning complex domains or long-lived service portfolios.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to frame research around behavior, synthesize interview data into mental spaces, map existing support, and expose gaps or misplaced investment.
