## Why it matters

The User Experience Team of One belongs in Design Books because it helps small teams practice useful UX work without waiting for a large research or design organization. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for UX designers, product teams, and engineers; freelancers, studio designers, and design leads; researchers, product designers, and founders. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with clearer interfaces and better diagnosis of friction; stronger client process, facilitation, and judgment; better questions, interviews, synthesis, and evidence. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
