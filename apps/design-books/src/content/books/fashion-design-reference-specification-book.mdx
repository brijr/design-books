## Why it matters

Fashion brings materials, bodies, manufacturing, culture, and commerce into one design practice. This reference broadens the collection beyond screens and graphics while keeping the focus on usable craft knowledge.

## Best for

Best for fashion students, multidisciplinary designers, and creative practitioners who want a concise map of the field.

## What you'll learn

You will gain working vocabulary for collections, textiles, construction, presentation, and the path from an idea to a finished garment.
