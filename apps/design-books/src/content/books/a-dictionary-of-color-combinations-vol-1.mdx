## Why it matters

Wada treats a palette as a relationship, not a shopping list of isolated colors. The book preserves 348 combinations from his influential Japanese color studies, making historical material immediately useful for contemporary identity, editorial, interface, fashion, and interior work.

## Best for

Best for designers who want a compact analog reference beside the desk. It is particularly useful when a project needs a palette with more character and historical texture than a conventional digital color picker tends to produce.

## What you'll learn

You will build a sharper eye for proportion, contrast, and unexpected harmony. The combinations offer starting points rather than rules, encouraging you to study why several colors hold together before adapting them to a new context.
