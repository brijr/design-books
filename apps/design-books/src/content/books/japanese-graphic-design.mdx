## Why it matters

This survey adds a broad view of contemporary Japanese practice to a collection already strong in Japanese design philosophy. The range makes visible how tradition, commercial culture, craft, and experimentation coexist.

## Best for

Best for graphic and identity designers seeking cross-cultural references across print, packaging, exhibitions, and brand systems.

## What you'll learn

You will encounter varied approaches to type, image, material, pacing, and composition—and gain references beyond the familiar canonical names.
