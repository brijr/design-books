## Why it matters

Judd's work is a rigorous education in relationships: object and room, surface and light, repetition and variation. It sharpens visual judgment without turning the work into a set of stylistic recipes.

## Best for

Best for spatial, industrial, and graphic designers interested in reduction, serial systems, and the behavior of material and color.

## What you'll learn

You will develop a closer eye for proportion, interval, fabrication, and the way an object changes the space around it.
