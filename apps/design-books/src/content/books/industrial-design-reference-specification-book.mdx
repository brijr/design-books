## Why it matters

This reference grounds product design in physical constraints: materials, processes, safety, dimensions, and production. It gives the collection a practical bridge from concept development to manufactured objects.

## Best for

Best for industrial and product designers, students, and digital designers expanding into hardware or connected products.

## What you'll learn

You will build a practical vocabulary for research, form development, manufacturing choices, human factors, and communicating a product for production.
