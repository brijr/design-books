## Why it matters

Berger shows that seeing is never neutral. The book gives designers a language for questioning how framing, reproduction, status, and social power influence what an image appears to say.

## Best for

Best for anyone who selects, composes, commissions, or critiques images—and wants to look beyond surface aesthetics.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to ask who made an image, for whom, under what conditions, and how its meaning changes when it moves between contexts.
