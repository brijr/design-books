## Why it matters

Ware connects visualization choices to what the visual system can detect, group, compare, and remember. It supplies deeper perceptual evidence for a collection rich in applied information design.

## Best for

Best for data-visualization practitioners, researchers, and interface designers working on dense or interactive displays.

## What you'll learn

You will understand preattentive features, color perception, spatial organization, pattern, attention, and how these mechanisms affect the speed and accuracy of interpretation.
