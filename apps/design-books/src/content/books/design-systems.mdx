## Why it matters

Design Systems belongs in Design Books because it extends the systems shelf with practical guidance for maintaining coherent components, patterns, and decisions. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for design systems teams, product designers, and engineers; product designers, founders, and product teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with repeatable structures that make design work coherent; more useful products and better experience decisions. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
