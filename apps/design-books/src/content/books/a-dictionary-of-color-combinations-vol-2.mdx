## Why it matters

Volume 2 broadens Wada's color archive with palettes organized around the Japanese seasons and combinations drawn from early twentieth-century applied design. Its range makes the set useful both as design history and as a practical source of unfamiliar visual relationships.

## Best for

Best for brand, graphic, product, and interior designers looking for palettes that feel specific rather than algorithmic. The removable color chips also make it especially useful for hands-on comparison and composition.

## What you'll learn

You will see how season, material, and cultural context can shape a color system. Used alongside Volume 1, it becomes a generous vocabulary for testing mood, hierarchy, and balance across several colors at once.
