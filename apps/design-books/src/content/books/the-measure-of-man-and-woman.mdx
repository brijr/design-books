## Why it matters

Design claims to be human-centered, but physical products and environments require actual knowledge of bodies. This reference makes anthropometric evidence available at the moment dimensions become decisions.

## Best for

Best for industrial, spatial, furniture, and interface designers working across physical reach, posture, controls, or environments.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to design from ranges rather than an imagined average, compare physical constraints, and recognize where measurement must be paired with inclusive research.
