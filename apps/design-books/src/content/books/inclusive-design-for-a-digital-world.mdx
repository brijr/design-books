## Why it matters

Gilbert frames accessibility as an end-to-end design responsibility rather than a final compliance check. This updated edition brings inclusive practice into design systems, AI, extended reality, gaming, and organizational delivery.

## Best for

Best for designers, developers, researchers, and product leaders who need accessibility embedded throughout the product lifecycle.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to include disabled participants in research, work with assistive technologies, apply standards thoughtfully, and build inclusive patterns into systems and team practice.
