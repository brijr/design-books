## Why it matters

Few projects show the public value of systematic graphic design as clearly as this railway manual. Its language-independent approach connects identity, wayfinding, and everyday service delivery.

## Best for

Best for information designers, wayfinding teams, transit designers, and anyone building a visual system that must work consistently at scale.

## What you'll learn

You will learn how grids, pictograms, naming, and placement rules become an understandable environment rather than a collection of isolated signs.
