## Why it matters

The Shape of Design belongs in Design Books because it brings a contemporary voice to design craft, helping readers connect process, judgment, and meaning. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for designers who want sharper judgment and context; designers, writers, artists, and creative teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with better language for explaining why design decisions matter; stronger creative habits and sharper ways of seeing. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
