## Why it matters

Maps can be decorative deliverables or working models that align a team. Kalbach focuses on the latter, showing how diagrams connect evidence about people's experiences to decisions about services and organizations.

## Best for

Best for service designers, researchers, facilitators, and product leaders tackling journeys that cross teams and channels.

## What you'll learn

You will learn when to use journey maps, service blueprints, experience maps, mental-model diagrams, and ecosystem maps—and how to build them collaboratively.
