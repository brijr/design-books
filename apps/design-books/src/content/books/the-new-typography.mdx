## Why it matters

This is not merely a style guide; it is a historical argument about how communication should respond to new technology and social conditions. Its certainty remains provocative and useful to examine.

## Best for

Best for designers studying the roots of modern typography, manifestos, and the relationship between technological change and visual form.

## What you'll learn

You will understand the reasoning behind asymmetry, standardization, hierarchy, and functional layout—and see how design principles carry the assumptions of their era.
