## Why it matters

This focused collection shows how designers compress buildings and spatial ideas into memorable symbols. Its narrow subject makes comparison easy and reveals recurring solutions across eras and regions.

## Best for

Best for identity designers, architects, and anyone researching visual metaphors for place, structure, and the built environment.

## What you'll learn

You will see how silhouette, perspective, geometry, and negative space turn architectural detail into a scalable mark.
