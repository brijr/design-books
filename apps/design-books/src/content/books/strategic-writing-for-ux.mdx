## Why it matters

Podmajersky connects individual words to product goals, repeatable patterns, and measurement. The second edition also addresses the new content-design problems introduced by generative interfaces.

## Best for

Best for UX writers, content designers, product designers, and teams building a shared system for interface language.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to define voice, design conversations, apply text patterns, edit for action, research effectiveness, and plan language for LLM-based products.
