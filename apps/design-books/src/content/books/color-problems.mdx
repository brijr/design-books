## Why it matters

Vanderpoel transformed observations of objects and environments into structured fields of color decades before similar visual strategies became canonical. The facsimile expands both the history and methods represented in the collection.

## Best for

Best for color researchers, artists, graphic designers, and information designers who value careful observation and unusual historical sources.

## What you'll learn

You will see how colors can be isolated, compared, proportioned, and translated into analytical diagrams without losing their sensory character.
