## Why it matters

The book updates formal design education for a world of motion, code, and systems while retaining careful attention to visual fundamentals. Its student work turns each concept into something observable and testable.

## Best for

Best for students, self-taught designers, and experienced practitioners who want a structured refresher on visual form.

## What you'll learn

You will practice seeing point, line, plane, scale, hierarchy, texture, color, layers, transparency, and modularity as connected design variables.
