## Why it matters

Pater makes visible the politics inside apparently ordinary design decisions. It strengthens the collection's critical perspective by connecting visual form to language, borders, economics, and identity.

## Best for

Best for graphic and information designers working across cultures, especially those responsible for public communication.

## What you'll learn

You will become better at identifying hidden defaults, testing claims of universality, and considering who gains or loses clarity from a design choice.
