## Why it matters

Living with Complexity belongs in Design Books because it helps designers separate necessary complexity from confusion and make complicated systems feel manageable. It helps readers connect Product Design, Usability and UX to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for product designers, founders, and product teams; UX designers, product teams, and engineers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with more useful products and clearer experience decisions; clearer interfaces and better diagnosis of user friction. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
