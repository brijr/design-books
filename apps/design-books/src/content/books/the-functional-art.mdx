## Why it matters

Cairo rejects the false choice between beauty and function in visualization. The book gives the collection an accessible bridge from graphic form to evidence, explanation, and editorial responsibility.

## Best for

Best for information designers, journalists, analysts, and product teams that communicate quantitative or complex material.

## What you'll learn

You will learn to choose forms based on reader goals, structure information before styling it, and critique graphics for both truthfulness and usability.
