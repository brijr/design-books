## Why it matters

Virgil Abloh changed how design, fashion, music, and popular culture speak to one another. Givhan's account adds the institutions and social context needed to understand that influence beyond individual objects.

## Best for

Best for designers interested in cultural authorship, creative careers, fashion, and how new voices enter established institutions.

## What you'll learn

You will see how collaboration, quotation, networks, and public identity can become design methods—and how questions of access shape what culture recognizes as design.
