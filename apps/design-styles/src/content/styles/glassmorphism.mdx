## Origins

2020. Big Sur makes frost a system default again. Marketing sites copy the panel until the panel is a trend name. It sits on Flat Design: the same grid, a translucent layer, a 1px light edge.

It is not skeuomorphism. The panel is not a window you could open. It is not Y2K. The glass is not candy. It is a way to stack UI without going back to leather.

The look dates fast when the blur is the only idea. It lasts when it is a system material — used for what sits above, not for everything.

## In the wild

System settings, dashboards, every "premium" marketing card in 2021. Still current in OS chrome. Less current as a whole site.

The cheap version is a blur and a hope. The real thing needs a background worth frosting and a reason for the stack.
