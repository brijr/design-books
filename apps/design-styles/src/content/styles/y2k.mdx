## Origins

The window is short: late nineties into the first years of the new century. The millennium was a deadline and a party. Interfaces, ads, and splash pages tried to look like the year they were entering.

Then skeuomorphism kept the materials and lost the candy. Flat design threw the materials out. The revival — fashion first, then interfaces — starts around 2018 and treats the original as a sample pack: chrome type, gel, stars, aqua.

There is no founder. The look lived in Flash intros, CD-ROM menus, Nokia wallpapers, and one operating system that made a gel button the default.

## In the wild

Revived in streetwear, in "cyber" beauty, and in interfaces that want nostalgia without going ironic. A gel button on a dark purple field is the field mark.

The revival is easy to overdo. One wet button is a citation. A page of lens flare is a costume.
