## Origins

Late 2010s tech marketing. Facebook's Alegria is the famous house style. Everyone else copies the noodle arm, the coral and mint, the scene of a person who has never had a joint.

The name is a joke that stuck, and a confusion. It has almost nothing to do with Milan Memphis except a willingness to use pastel. The job is to make a platform look human without showing a human.

It is already a period style. The backlash arrived on time: too smooth, too same, too willing to stand in for the work. It is still the default empty state.

## In the wild

Onboarding, empty states, conference walls, the "our values" page. Easy to commission, hard to retire.

The cheap version is a blob and a laptop. The real tell is a whole world in which nobody has a job, only a gesture.
