## Origins

London and New York, 1976. The music is fast. The graphic has to be faster. A copier, a knife, a face from a newspaper. Reid makes the national version. Everyone else makes the local one.

It is Dada without the cabaret — the same cut, a different enemy. The industry, the palace, the tasteful album cover.

The method outlives the first bands. Zines, riot grrrl, every basement show. The through-line is the copier as a press.

## In the wild

Gig posters that still look like they were made tonight. Fashion that wants danger without a venue. Brand campaigns that buy a ransom font.

The cheap version is a distressed texture. The real thing looks like it might fall off the wall.
