## Origins

Early 1990s, American print. Carson at Ray Gun, Chantry in Seattle, a generation that had Photoshop and a distrust of the clean page. The music is called grunge; the graphic borrows the name because the dirt matches.

It is punk after software. The copier streak can be a filter. The layer can be infinite. That is why it spreads and why it dates: the texture becomes a preset.

By the late 1990s the look is everywhere and then nowhere. The web wants to be readable. The magazine wants to sell ads. The dirt washes off.

## In the wild

Band merch, "authentic" coffee, any brand that wants 1993. The filter is still in every app.

The cheap version is a distress slider. The real thing looks like a specific object that has been through a specific life.
