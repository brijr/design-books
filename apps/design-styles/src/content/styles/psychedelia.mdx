## Origins

San Francisco, 1966. The Fillmore, the Avalon, a weekly poster that had to stop you on the street and then refuse to give you the information too quickly. Wilson, Moscoso, Kelley, Tuten.

It is Art Nouveau after electricity and after LSD — the same liquid line, a different chemistry. Complementary colors sit next to each other until they buzz. Type hides in the ornament so that reading becomes a delay.

It commercializes immediately. Peter Max, advertising, the look as a product. The concert poster remains the field mark.

## In the wild

Festival branding, "retro" rock merch, any packaging that wants 1967 without the concert.

The cheap version is a groovy font. The real thing vibrates. Your eye should not be comfortable.
