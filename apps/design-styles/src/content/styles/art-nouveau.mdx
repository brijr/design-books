## Origins

Paris, Brussels, Vienna, Glasgow — roughly 1890 to 1910. A style with many names (Jugendstil, Modernisme, Sezession) and one graphic idea: the line that will not sit still.

It is the first modern poster style that treats the whole sheet as a designed surface. Lithography made the color possible. The department store and the theatre made the audience.

It dies fast. By 1910 the curve looks old. Geometry and the machine take the century. The line comes back whenever a generation wants nature without realism — psychedelia, then every boutique wine label.

## In the wild

Mucha is still the default "elegant poster." Métro ironwork. Packaging that wants to look European and pre-war without being specific.

The cheap version is a swirl and a serif. The real thing is a single line that refuses to break.
