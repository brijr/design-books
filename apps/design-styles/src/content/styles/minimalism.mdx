## Origins

As a graphic idea, reduction is old — Bauhaus, Swiss posters, Rams. As an interface style it becomes default after skeuomorphism recedes. iOS 7, early Material, and a generation of marketing pages that equate white space with trust.

The word is a problem. Minimalism names a 1960s art movement, a lifestyle brand, and a way of setting type. This entry is the visual language: few elements, open space, one family, one accent. The ethic lives next door.

It is easy to date. Pre-2013 software wanted to look like objects. Post-2013 software wanted to look like nothing in particular — which is itself a look.

## In the wild

Still the default prestige language of software and fashion. Easy to fake: delete until it looks expensive, even when the system underneath is noisy.

A fashion lookbook and a Series A homepage can share a file. The difference is whether the reduction is a decision or a template.
