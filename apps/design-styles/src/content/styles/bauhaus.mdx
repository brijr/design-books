## Origins

The school lasted fourteen years and three cities. Weimar 1919, Dessau 1925, Berlin 1932, closed 1933. Gropius's founding claim was not a look. It was a program: painting, typography, furniture, and architecture in one building, taught as one method.

The Vorkurs came first. Students learned seeing — material, contrast, composition — before they specialized. Itten, then Moholy-Nagy, then Albers. The preliminary course is why Bauhaus graphics still read as exercises that escaped the classroom.

It absorbed De Stijl's primaries and Constructivism's diagonal energy, then put them to work on letterheads, exhibitions, and housing. The afterlife is larger than the school: Ulm, Swiss typography, and a century of interfaces that treat the grid as a moral good.

## In the wild

Every tech wordmark that is a geometric sans on white. Every poster that leads with a circle and a bar. Museum graphics that want to look like they were designed in 1926.

The tell in contemporary UI is not the palette. It is the belief that reducing a screen to geometry makes it honest. Sometimes it does. Sometimes it is only a mood board of the school.
