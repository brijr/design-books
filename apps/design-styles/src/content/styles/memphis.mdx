## Origins

The group formed around Sottsass in Milan, showed in 1981, and was effectively over by the end of the decade. The name is a joke with two referents: Memphis, Egypt, and "Stuck Inside of Mobile with the Memphis Blues Again," left playing at an early meeting.

Sottsass had already designed the Olivetti Valentine. Memphis was the refusal of that career's good manners — a way to put decoration, humor, and cheap-looking luxury back on the table after decades of good taste as law.

The first collection was furniture, lighting, and ceramics. Graphics followed the same grammar because the surfaces demanded it. Pattern was not applied to an object. The object was a pattern that had learned to stand up.

## In the wild

Hotel lobbies that want to signal "design." Album covers that need a 1980s without neon. Any brand that pairs a squiggle with a triangle and hopes you will feel joy.

The revival is constant because the original was already a revival of everything it could steal. When you see Memphis in 2026 it is usually a quote. The quote is still louder than most of the room.
