## Origins

The desktop had always borrowed the desk. The iPhone made the borrowing a style: Notes on legal paper, iBooks on a wooden shelf, Game Center on green felt. 2007 to 2013 is the window most people mean.

The argument is kindness. A new device should look like something you already know how to touch. The risk is a costume. A calendar does not need to be leather to be a calendar.

iOS 7 ends the era in public. Flat Design is the refusal. The metaphors remain in places — a trash can, a toggle that still looks like a switch.

## In the wild

Rare as a full system. Common as a leftover: the recorder that looks like a mic, the notebook app that still rules a line.

A revival would look like a joke or a craft. The original did not know it was a period style. That is the tell.
