## Origins

New York after the war. European modernists and American magazines in the same city. Rand, Bass, Pineles, Lubalin — not a school with a manifesto so much as a scene with clients.

They keep the European inheritance: sans, photography, the clean page. They add what Switzerland often refused — illustration, humor, the concept you can tell someone in a sentence.

It is the graphic of the American century's confidence. Record sleeves, title sequences, corporate marks that want to be clever and durable at once.

## In the wild

Every startup mark that wants to be "smart." Every film title that quotes Bass. Magazine covers that still believe in an idea.

The cheap version is a wink. The real thing is a thought you can redraw from memory.
