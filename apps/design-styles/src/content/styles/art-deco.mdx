## Origins

Paris, 1925, the exposition that gave the style its later name. It is Nouveau's grandchild and Constructivism's cousin in a tuxedo. Geometry, but for glamour. The machine, but as jewelry.

Cassandre's posters are the graphic peak: the liner, the train, the letterform as a building. The look travels — ocean liners, radio cabinets, type specimens, hotel façades.

It never really dies. Every decade that wants "glamour" without Victorian clutter reaches for a sunburst and a stepped rule. The revival is the default.

## In the wild

Hotel branding, champagne, anything that wants 1925 without the politics. Streamlined logos that are actually Deco quotes.

The cheap version is a cheap gold gradient. The real thing has weight — the step, the fan, the letter that could hold a cornice.
