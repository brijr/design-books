## Origins

Metro was early. iOS 7 made it a public event. 2013 is the year most people mean: leather and felt leave, color planes arrive, icons become signs.

It is Swiss Style for a phone — sans, module, a poster that you hold. It is also a production decision. Textures are expensive to maintain. A fill is not.

The look is still the default. Glassmorphism and neubrutalism are reactions, not replacements. Most software is still a set of flat planes with a little radius.

## In the wild

Every settings screen. Every onboarding. Every "system" that wants to look like iOS or Material without saying so.

The cheap version is a bright rectangle and a thin icon. The real thing is a system: type, space, and color that can survive a hundred screens.
