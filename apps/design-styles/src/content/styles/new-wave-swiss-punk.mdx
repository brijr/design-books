## Origins

Basel, late 1970s. Weingart teaches the Swiss method and then lets the letter step off the line. The students export it. Greiman takes it to California and to the Macintosh.

It is not punk. It is too skilled. It is not Swiss Style. It is too willing to collide. The name "Swiss Punk" is a joke that stuck because it names the parent and the crime.

The Macintosh makes the method cheaper. Layer, overlap, a letter you can drag. The craft remains: you can still see that someone knew the grid.

## In the wild

Art-school posters, early digital magazines, any identity that wants "expressive" without leaving the grotesque.

The cheap version is a random stagger. The real thing still adds up. You can reconstruct the grid it broke.
