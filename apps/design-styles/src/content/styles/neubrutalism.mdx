## Origins

The name is a tell: this is not 1950s béton brut, and it is not the "brutalist websites" catalog of unstyled pages. It is a 2010s–20s interface style that took the anti-polish stance and turned it into a kit — border, offset, saturated fill.

It arrives after a decade of soft UI: blur, pale gradients, ghost buttons. The reaction is a box that admits it is a box. Gumroad-era marketing made the kit famous. Portfolios and indie tools copied the card until the card became a default.

## In the wild

Still current on indie product sites, design-tool marketing, and portfolios that want to look handmade without looking unfinished.

The risk is the same as any kit. A hard shadow is not a personality. When every section uses the same offset, the refusal of polish becomes a polish of its own.
