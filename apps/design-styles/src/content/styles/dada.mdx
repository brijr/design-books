## Origins

Zurich, 1916, in a war the rest of Europe was busy dying in. Cabaret Voltaire, manifestos, and pages that would not sit still. Berlin made it political. Paris made it literary. New York made it an import.

The graphic method is collage and chance. You cut the newspaper. You rotate the type. You let the accident stay. Sense is the enemy because sense had just organized a slaughter.

Constructivism takes the diagonal and puts it to work. Punk takes the ransom letter and puts it on a flyer. The through-line is the page as an attack.

## In the wild

Every "disrupted" campaign that scatters type and calls it energy. Zines. Album covers that want to look like they were assembled in an hour.

Most citations are polite. Dada was not. If the collage still reads as a layout, it has already lost.
