## Origins

The Netherlands, 1917. A magazine, a manifesto, and a handful of people who thought the right angle could hold a world. Mondrian painted it. Van Doesburg published it. Rietveld built it.

The war had made the old pictures look like a lie. De Stijl's answer is not collage. It is reduction until only relation remains — horizontal, vertical, primary.

It feeds the Bauhaus and then every later "universal" graphic. The chair and the painting are still the easiest way to teach a child what modern looks like.

## In the wild

Museum shops. CSS Grid demos. Any brand that wants "modern" and reaches for a red rectangle.

The cheap version is a Mondrian cake. The real thing is a balance so tight that moving one bar would collapse it.
