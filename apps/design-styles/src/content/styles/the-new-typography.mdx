## Origins

Germany, mid-1920s. Tschichold writes the book that turns Constructivist energy into something a jobbing printer can follow. Flush left. Sans-serif. Photography. No centered title in a wreath.

It is a method, not a school. That is why it spreads. You do not need to have been in Dessau. You need the manual and a case of Futura.

Tschichold later recants the severity. The Swiss pick it up and make it stricter. The through-line is the asymmetric page as the modern default.

## In the wild

Every "clean" annual report that still uses a red rule. Every publisher who set a cover in Futura and thought that was the whole job.

The method is now so default that it is invisible. That is a kind of victory. It is also why the original book still startles — it is arguing for something you thought was weather.
