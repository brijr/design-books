## Origins

Switzerland, 1950s. Basel and Zurich. Designers who had inherited the New Typography and wanted a system you could teach, export, and hand to a client in another country.

The grid is the invention that matters. Not a suggestion — a modular law. Akzidenz-Grotesk, then Helvetica. Photography as evidence. The designer as an engineer of reading.

It becomes the default prestige language of corporations and airports. Then a generation in the late 1970s breaks it on purpose. The break is how you know the system had won.

## In the wild

Transit maps, pharmaceutical identities, every "timeless" rebrand. The look is so default that people call it "clean" and think they have said something.

A real citation shows the module. A fake one sets Helvetica and hopes.
