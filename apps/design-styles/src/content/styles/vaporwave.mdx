## Origins

The internet, early 2010s. A music scene and a folder of images: Greek busts, Windows chrome, mall fountains, Japanese retail, pink and cyan. The 1980s and 1990s as a ruined advertisement.

It is not Y2K. Y2K still wants to sell you the future. Vaporwave is what the future looks like after it has been left in a mall.

Imagery policy matters here. Recreate the dusk, the grid, the bust. Do not lift the record, the logo, or the game sprite. The style is a mood, not an archive of other people's assets.

## In the wild

Album covers, fashion, the occasional "aesthetic" landing page. Easy to costume, hard to mean.

The cheap version is a bust and a gradient. The real thing feels like a place that has already closed.
