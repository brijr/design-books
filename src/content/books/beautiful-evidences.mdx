## Why it matters

Beautiful Evidences belongs in Design Books because it shows how visual evidence can clarify arguments, reveal patterns, and respect the intelligence of the reader. It helps readers connect Information Design, Visual Perception to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers who work with evidence, data, and explanation; visual designers, researchers, and educators. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with ways to make complex material easier to inspect; a sharper understanding of how people organize visual form. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
