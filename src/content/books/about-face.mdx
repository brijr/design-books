## Why it matters

About Face belongs in Design Books because it adds a heavyweight interaction design reference for connecting user goals to screens, flows, and behavior. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for product designers, founders, and product teams; UX designers, product teams, and engineers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with more useful products and better experience decisions; clearer interfaces and better diagnosis of friction. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
