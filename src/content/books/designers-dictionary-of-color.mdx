## Why it matters

The Designer's Dictionary of Color belongs in Design Books because it turns color selection into a more informed design decision by connecting palettes to history and association. It helps readers connect Color, Graphic Design to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for visual designers, illustrators, and interface designers; graphic designers, art directors, and visual designers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with more intentional palettes and better sensitivity to context; clearer hierarchy, composition, and communication. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
