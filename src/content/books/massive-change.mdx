## Why it matters

Massive Change belongs in Design Books because it expands design from objects to systems, asking how technology, policy, and culture shape possible futures. It helps readers connect Design Theory, Product Design to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers who want sharper critical judgment; product designers, founders, and product teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with better language for explaining why a design choice works; more useful products and clearer experience decisions. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
