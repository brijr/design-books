## Why it matters

Creative Confidence belongs in Design Books because it treats creativity as a repeatable practice that teams can develop through prototypes, empathy, and momentum. It helps readers connect Creative Practice, Product Design to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers, writers, artists, and creative teams; product designers, founders, and product teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with healthier creative habits and stronger momentum; more useful products and clearer experience decisions. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
