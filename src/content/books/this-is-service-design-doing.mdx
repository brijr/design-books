## Why it matters

This Is Service Design Doing belongs in Design Books because it adds hands-on service design methods for working across people, channels, workshops, and delivery constraints. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for service designers, product teams, and facilitators; freelancers, studio designers, and design leads. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with clearer journeys, touchpoints, and cross-channel systems; stronger client process, facilitation, and judgment. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
