## Why it matters

Design as Art belongs in Design Books because it expands design beyond style by treating everyday objects, communication, and culture as material for judgment. It helps readers connect Design Theory, Design History to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers who want sharper critical judgment; designers who want historical reference and context. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with better language for explaining why a design choice works; reference points that make current design choices easier to judge. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
