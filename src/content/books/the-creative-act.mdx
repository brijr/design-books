## Why it matters

The Creative Act: A Way of Being belongs in Design Books because it treats creativity as a way of paying attention, editing carefully, and staying receptive to unexpected material. It helps readers connect Creative Practice to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers, writers, artists, and creative teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with healthier creative habits and stronger momentum. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
