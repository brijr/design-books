## Why it matters

Atomic Design belongs in Design Books because it frames interface design as a modular system, helping teams connect components to complete product experiences. It helps readers connect Design Systems, Product Design, Usability and UX to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for product designers, design systems teams, and engineers; product designers, founders, and product teams; UX designers, product teams, and engineers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with repeatable structures that keep design work coherent; more useful products and clearer experience decisions; clearer interfaces and better diagnosis of user friction. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
