## Why it matters

Designing Programmes belongs in Design Books because it shows how rules and variation can generate rich graphic outcomes without sacrificing clarity or control. It helps readers connect Design Systems, Graphic Design to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for product designers, design systems teams, and engineers; graphic designers, art directors, and visual designers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with repeatable structures that keep design work coherent; clearer hierarchy, composition, and communication. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
