## Why it matters

A *New* Program for Graphic Design belongs in Design Books because it turns graphic design fundamentals into a sequence of lessons that connect history, form, and studio practice. It helps readers connect Graphic Design, Design Theory, Typography to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for graphic designers, art directors, and visual designers; designers who want sharper critical judgment; typographers, editorial designers, and interface designers. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with clearer hierarchy, composition, and communication; better language for explaining why a design choice works; more careful type choices and stronger reading systems. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
