## Why it matters

Interaction of Color belongs in Design Books because it makes color relational, showing how perception shifts when hue, value, proportion, and context change. It helps readers connect Color, Visual Perception to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for visual designers, illustrators, and interface designers; visual designers, researchers, and educators. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with more intentional palettes and better sensitivity to context; a sharper understanding of how people organize visual form. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
