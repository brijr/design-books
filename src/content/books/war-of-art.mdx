## Why it matters

The War of Art belongs in Design Books because it focuses on resistance, discipline, and the daily decision to do serious creative work despite friction. It helps readers connect Creative Practice, Professional Practice to the choices they make in real design work.

## Best for

Best for designers, writers, artists, and creative teams; freelancers, studio designers, and design leads. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment without changing the visual shape of your work.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with healthier creative habits and stronger momentum; better client process, critique, and studio judgment. The value is not just inspiration; it is a stronger vocabulary for critique, selection, and follow-through.
