## Why it matters

Virgil Abloh: Figures of Speech belongs in Design Books because it documents how Abloh moved between fashion, art, branding, objects, and visual systems with a highly referential design language. It expands the list without duplicating the books already covering the same ground.

## Best for

Best for graphic designers, art directors, and visual designers; designers, writers, artists, and creative teams. The book is useful when you want reference material that improves judgment and gives teams more precise working language.

## What you'll learn

You will come away with clearer composition, hierarchy, and visual communication; stronger creative habits and sharper ways of seeing. The value is practical: better critique, better choices, and better follow-through.
