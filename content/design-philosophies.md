# design-philosophies.com

Working document for what this site becomes. Sibling of design-books.com —
same care, its own face.

## Premise

_One line: what is this site?_ (draft) The schools of thought behind good
design — the principles, credos, and worldviews designers actually work
from, each presented in its own voice.

## Why this site

Principles get quoted ("less but better") far more than understood. There's
no single well-made home for design's philosophies: where each came from,
what it actually claims, what it argues against, and what it looks like
applied.

## Audience

Designers forming a point of view; anyone who wants the "why" layer that
sits above styles (how it looks) and books (where it's written down).

## Content model

_What is one entry?_ (sketch) A **philosophy**: name, originator/lineage,
core claims (in plain words), what it opposes, canonical texts (cross-link
design-books!), exemplary work, critiques. E.g. Rams' ten principles,
Bauhaus's unity of art and industry, wabi-sabi, "form follows function,"
user-centered design, calm technology, brutalist honesty of materials.

## Seed entries
