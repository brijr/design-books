# Lock the design-styles brief and launch list

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02

## Question

Turn the family vision's design-styles section into a locked one-page brief
plus a ~20-entry launch list in `content/design-styles.md`. The vision
settles the premise, the movement/style content types, the attribute set, the
specimen page anatomy, and the timeline organization — this ticket grills
what's left:

- Imagery strategy, given what the rights research found (sourced specimens
  vs original recreations vs hybrid) — this shapes what "annotated visual
  examples" can actually be.
- Medium scope at launch: all five mediums (graphic, editorial, identity,
  interface, product) or a narrower opening slice?
- The launch list itself: which ~20 movements/styles, balancing eras,
  mediums, and the timeline's spine (drafted by the agent, chosen by
  Bridger).
- Voice, and anything the prior-art survey says to position against.

Resolution: the brief locked in `content/design-styles.md`, launch list
included.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-08-14 via grilling (two rounds). Brief locked in
[`content/design-styles.md`](../../content/design-styles.md). Decisions:

- **Imagery policy**: the research strategy adopted verbatim — recreations
  first, CC0 second, fair use sparingly; `source` + `license` per image.
- **Recreations are medium-matched**: live HTML/CSS/SVG specimens for
  digitally native styles (the specimen IS the style, inspectable); annotated
  diagrams + CC0 museum imagery for historical movements. The signature move.
- **Launch scope**: 2D-visual (graphic, identity, interface); editorial folds
  into graphic; product entries are a second wave.
- **Voice**: field-guide observational — recognition-first, "how to spot it,"
  a birding guide written by a designer.
- **Launch list**: all 24 drafted candidates kept (Bridger: "I like all of
  these"), timeline-grouped into Foundations / Modernism / Counterculture &
  Postmodernism / Digital & Living, with ★ proving anchors Bauhaus, Memphis,
  Y2K, Minimalism, Neubrutalism. Bench recorded for post-launch.
