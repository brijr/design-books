# Road to 100: the next 21 design books

Research date: 2026-08-14

## Count and selection logic

The catalog audit started from **66 published entries**. The shelf photos, Gmail order history, and the current add list account for **13 owned or on-order additions**, bringing the planned catalog to **79**. The **21 recommendations below bring the plan to exactly 100**:

`66 current + 13 owned/on-order + 21 recommended = 100`

The 13 already accounted for are intentionally excluded here: *IBM Graphic Design Guide from 1969 to 1987*; *Passenger Information System: Design Manual for Swiss Federal Railways*; *Fashion Design Reference & Specification Book*; *Industrial Design Reference & Specification Book*; *Make It Ours*; *A Philosophy of Software Design* (2nd ed.); *Abloh-isms*; *A Dictionary of Color Combinations* Vols. 1 and 2; *Donald Judd: Artworks 1970–1994*; *Architectural Logos*; *1,000 Marks by Pentagram*; and *Japanese Graphic Design*.

This is a catalog plan, not a claim that every title is already physically on the shelf: the 13-book subtotal includes books still on order.

The current collection is strongest in product design (23 tags), UX (17), graphic design (17), and design theory (13). Its thinnest useful shelves are systems thinking (1), service design (2), content design (2), inclusive design (2), color (2), design research (3), and typography (4). The slate therefore keeps the collection's modernist, Swiss, and visually rigorous character while deliberately strengthening those thinner areas. It reuses the site's existing topic slugs; no new taxonomy is needed.

## Shelf 1 — Modernist foundations and graphic lineages

### 1. *The Vignelli Canon*

- **Massimo Vignelli** — Lars Müller Publishers, 2010 — ISBN `9783037782255`
- **Why:** The clearest small bridge between the collection's grids, logos, corporate systems, typography, and disciplined modernist practice. It is the highest-confidence fit for this particular shelf.
- **Topics:** `graphic-design`, `typography`, `design-theory`, `brand-identity`
- **Primary source:** [Lars Müller Publishers](https://www.lars-mueller-publishers.com/vignelli-canon)

### 2. *Typography: A Manual of Design*

- **Emil Ruder** — Niggli, 1967 — ISBN `9783721200430`
- **Why:** Pairs naturally with Müller-Brockmann's *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* while going deeper on rhythm, proportion, readability, and type itself. Niggli records 1967 for the multilingual edition it currently sells.
- **Topics:** `typography`, `graphic-design`, `design-history`, `visual-perception`
- **Primary source:** [Niggli](https://niggli.ch/en/products/typographie)

### 3. *The New Typography*

- **Jan Tschichold**, translated by Ruari McLean — University of California Press, 2006 edition — ISBN `9780520250123`
- **Why:** Adds the machine-age argument behind the modernist layouts already represented visually across the collection. It gives the shelf a foundational primary text, not only retrospective surveys.
- **Topics:** `typography`, `graphic-design`, `design-history`, `design-theory`
- **Primary source:** [University of California Press](https://www.ucpress.edu/books/the-new-typography/paper)

### 4. *Swiss Graphic Design: The Origins and Growth of an International Style, 1920–1965*

- **Richard Hollis** — Laurence King, 2006 — ISBN `9781856694759`
- **Why:** Supplies the social and historical context around the collection's Swiss manuals, wayfinding systems, grids, Helvetica, and corporate identity books.
- **Topics:** `design-history`, `graphic-design`, `typography`, `brand-identity`
- **Primary sources:** [Hachette UK / Laurence King](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/richard-hollis/swiss-graphic-design-hb/9781856694759/), [Richard Hollis bibliography](https://www.richardhollis.com/bibliography/)

### 5. *Graphic Design: The New Basics*, Revised Second Edition

- **Ellen Lupton and Jennifer Cole Phillips** — Princeton Architectural Press, 2015 — ISBN `9781616893323`
- **Why:** Turns the collection's theory-heavy visual shelf into a teachable studio sequence covering hierarchy, grids, color, texture, data, typography, and Gestalt principles.
- **Topics:** `graphic-design`, `visual-perception`, `typography`, `color`
- **Primary source:** [Princeton Architectural Press](https://papress.com/products/graphic-design-the-new-basics-revised-second-edition-paperback)

### 6. *Meggs' History of Graphic Design*, 7th Edition

- **Philip B. Meggs, Alston W. Purvis, Sandra Maxa, and Mark Sanders** — Wiley, 2025 — ISBN `9781119743293`
- **Why:** Gives the collection a broad chronological spine against which its highly specific monographs and manuals can be placed. The current edition expands the global and thematic framing.
- **Topics:** `design-history`, `graphic-design`, `visual-perception`
- **Primary source:** [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Graphic%2BDesign-c-GD00)

### 7. *Ways of Seeing*

- **John Berger** — Penguin Books, 1990 paperback edition (original work 1972) — ISBN `9780140135152`
- **Why:** Adds critical visual literacy: how reproduction, advertising, gender, ownership, and cultural context shape what an image appears to mean. It is a useful counterweight to purely formal design instruction.
- **Topics:** `visual-perception`, `design-theory`, `design-history`
- **Primary source:** [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/324430/ways-of-seeing-by-john-berger/)

## Shelf 2 — Responsible visual communication, color, and information

### 8. *The Politics of Design: A (Not So) Global Manual for Visual Communication*

- **Ruben Pater** — BIS Publishers, 2016 — ISBN `9789063694227`
- **Why:** Extends *Design Justice* into daily visual choices—maps, symbols, typography, photography, and color—and makes the consequences of “neutral” communication concrete.
- **Topics:** `inclusive-design`, `graphic-design`, `design-theory`, `information-design`
- **Primary source:** [BIS Publishers](https://www.bispublishers.com/the-politics-of-design.html)

### 9. *Color Problems: A Practical Manual for the Lay Student of Color*

- **Emily Noyes Vanderpoel** — The Circadian Press / Sacred Bones, 2018 facsimile (original work 1903) — **ISBN not listed by the publisher**
- **Why:** Complements Albers and Wada with an unusually early, systematic, grid-based approach to color analysis whose plates anticipate later abstraction and information design.
- **Topics:** `color`, `visual-perception`, `design-history`, `information-design`
- **Primary source:** [Sacred Bones / The Circadian Press](https://www.sacredbonesrecords.com/products/emily-noyes-vanderpoel-color-problems)

### 10. *The Functional Art: An Introduction to Information Graphics and Visualization*

- **Alberto Cairo** — New Riders, 2012 — paperback ISBN `9780321834737`
- **Why:** Adds a practical creation process to a shelf that already contains Tufte: function, cognition, history, critique, and interactive information graphics.
- **Topics:** `information-design`, `visual-perception`, `design-research`
- **Primary source:** [Pearson / New Riders](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/functional-art-the-an-introduction-to-information-graphics-and-visualization/P200000008946/9780321834737)

### 11. *Information Visualization: Perception for Design*, 4th Edition

- **Colin Ware** — Morgan Kaufmann / Elsevier, 2019 — ISBN `9780128128756`
- **Why:** Deepens the evidence underneath color, contrast, salience, pattern, spatial perception, cognition, and interactive visualization.
- **Topics:** `information-design`, `visual-perception`, `color`
- **Primary source:** [Elsevier](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/information-visualization/ware/978-0-12-812875-6)

### 12. *The Measure of Man and Woman: Human Factors in Design*, Revised Edition

- **Alvin R. Tilley and Henry Dreyfuss Associates** — Wiley, 2001 — ISBN `9780471099550`
- **Why:** Brings physical ergonomics and human factors into a collection dominated by screens and visual form. It also creates a direct bridge to the owned industrial-design reference book.
- **Topics:** `product-design`, `inclusive-design`, `design-research`
- **Primary source:** [Wiley](https://uat.store.wiley.com/en-us/the-measure-of-man-and-woman-human-factors-in-design-revised-edition-p-9780471099550)

## Shelf 3 — Services, content, research, and humane products

### 13. *Good Services: How to Design Services That Work*

- **Lou Downe** — BIS Publishers, 2019 — ISBN `9789063695439`
- **Why:** A compact, durable set of principles for judging whole services rather than isolated screens—the clearest way to strengthen the site's very small service-design shelf.
- **Topics:** `service-design`, `systems-thinking`, `product-design`
- **Primary source:** [BIS Publishers](https://www.bispublishers.com/good-services.html)

### 14. *Content Design: Research, Plan and Deliver the Content Your Audience Wants and Needs*, 2nd Edition

- **Sarah Winters and Rachel Edwards** — Content Design London / Lulu Press, 2024 — ISBN `9781304640390`
- **Why:** Adds the complete user-research-to-content workflow that sits between information architecture and interface writing. The second edition is the current, expanded successor to Sarah Richards' 2017 book.
- **Topics:** `content-design`, `design-research`, `usability-and-ux`
- **Primary sources:** [Content Design London](https://checkout.contentdesign.london/products/content-design-by-sarah-winters-and-rachel-edwards), [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/shop/rachel-edwards-and-sarah-winters/content-design/paperback/product-rmgdwkg.html)

### 15. *Mapping Experiences*, 2nd Edition

- **James Kalbach** — O'Reilly Media, 2020 — ISBN `9781492076629`
- **Why:** Unifies journeys, service blueprints, mental-model diagrams, and ecosystem maps in a visual practice that suits the collection's systems orientation.
- **Topics:** `service-design`, `design-research`, `information-design`
- **Primary source:** [O'Reilly Media](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/mapping-experiences-2nd/9781492076629/)

### 16. *Mental Models: Aligning Design Strategy with Human Behavior*

- **Indi Young** — Rosenfeld Media, 2008 — paperback ISBN `9781933820064`
- **Why:** Adds a rigorous generative-research method for mapping people's motivations and locating gaps between behavior and product support.
- **Topics:** `design-research`, `systems-thinking`, `usability-and-ux`
- **Primary source:** [Rosenfeld Media](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/mental-models/)

### 17. *Strategic Writing for UX*, 2nd Edition

- **Torrey Podmajersky** — O'Reilly Media, 2025 — ISBN shown by the official digital catalog: `9781098174323`
- **Why:** Complements *Writing Is Designing* with operational text patterns, measurement, content systems, and updated guidance for AI-mediated products.
- **Topics:** `content-design`, `usability-and-ux`, `product-design`
- **Primary source:** [O'Reilly Media](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/strategic-writing-for/9781098174323/)

### 18. *Inclusive Design for a Digital World: Designing with Accessibility in Mind*, 2nd Edition

- **Reginé M. Gilbert** — Apress, 2025 — print ISBN `9798868818196`; ebook ISBN `9798868818202`
- **Why:** Makes accessibility an end-to-end practice spanning research, design systems, content architecture, WCAG 2.2, and emerging technology.
- **Topics:** `inclusive-design`, `usability-and-ux`, `design-research`
- **Primary source:** [Springer Nature / Apress](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/979-8-8688-1820-2)

### 19. *Design Meets Disability*

- **Graham Pullin** — The MIT Press, 2011 paperback (original hardcover 2009) — ISBN `9780262516747`
- **Why:** Connects inclusive design to industrial design, aesthetics, fashion, and assistive technology, expanding the topic beyond digital compliance.
- **Topics:** `inclusive-design`, `product-design`, `design-theory`
- **Primary source:** [The MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262516747/design-meets-disability/)

### 20. *Sketching User Experiences: Getting the Design Right and the Right Design*

- **Bill Buxton** — Morgan Kaufmann / Elsevier, 2007 — ISBN `9780123740373`
- **Why:** Adds a major practice-oriented bridge between creative exploration, interaction design, experience prototyping, and product development.
- **Topics:** `product-design`, `creative-practice`, `design-research`
- **Primary source:** [Elsevier](https://shop.elsevier.com/books/sketching-user-experiences-getting-the-design-right-and-the-right-design/buxton/978-0-12-374037-3)

### 21. *The Humane Interface: New Directions for Designing Interactive Systems*

- **Jef Raskin** — Addison-Wesley Professional, 2000 — ISBN `9780201379372`
- **Why:** Adds a foundational, opinionated interface text grounded in attention, habit formation, modes, navigation, and quantitative interaction analysis.
- **Topics:** `usability-and-ux`, `design-theory`, `product-design`
- **Primary source:** [InformIT / Pearson](https://www.informit.com/store/humane-interface-new-directions-for-designing-interactive-9780201379372)

## Suggested acquisition order

If these are purchased in waves, start with **1–5 and 13–18**: they best match the collection's visual identity while closing the most obvious topic gaps. Then add **8–12 and 19–21** for critical, inclusive, information, and physical-design depth. Use **6–7** last as broad contextual anchors; both are excellent, but they overlap more with material already represented across specialist books.
